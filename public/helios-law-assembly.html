From concept to evidence

A proposal is a starting point.

These miniature worlds illustrate one implementation path. The next step is to test costs, local rules, accessibility, feasibility and outcomes before deciding whether a proposal should proceed.

01 · Backyard homes

Measure accessory units completed and median housing costs. Verify fire access, habitability and utility capacity.

02 · Third places

Measure access to free gathering spaces and repeat attendance. Evaluate ongoing operating costs and equitable access.

03 · Safe water

Measure lead lines replaced and water lead test results. Verify inventories, cost and prioritization of high-exposure households.
