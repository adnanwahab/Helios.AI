HFL-090 / HGA-090 · Public Futures Simulation Act — editorial draft, not enacted law.

Inspect model assumptions and limitations

Fixed seed 90; 100 reproducible worlds. A simple integer hash gives each world capacity (35–100%), demand (0–1) and cost shock (0–1). Effective effort = chosen input × capacity. Housing and mobility indices = 100 + effective effort × 0.55 and 0.65 respectively − demand × effort × 0.12 and 0.17. Heat index = 100 − cooling effort × 0.42 + demand × cooling effort × 0.08. Cost index = 100 + sum of efforts × (0.14 + cost shock × 0.10). Baselines are normalized to 100 for the same world. This is a teaching model, not HappyBearLandia output, evidence, probability, financial estimate or a vote. No data is sent or saved. Real policies require validated data, uncertainty, budget and equity review.
