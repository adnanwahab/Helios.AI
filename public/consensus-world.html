Consensus World

Design a law for everyone.

Propose, simulate, listen, revise. Explore how the same synthetic population responds to each version of a bill, from 100,000 to eight billion individually evaluated agents.

A transparent toy model, not a prediction or public ballot. The agents are synthetic; simulated support never represents real consent. A good bill can have irreducible disagreement.

01 · Propose

The local rules recognize only a few explicit provisions. Review every assumption; equivalent wording should keep the same effects. Unknown is not evidence of benefit.

−2 = substantial modeled harm; +2 = substantial modeled benefit. 0 = neutral or unknown. All estimates are editable hypotheses.

WebGPU when available; CPU fallback is slower. Progress is checkpointed locally at completed batch boundaries. No API, no data submission.
