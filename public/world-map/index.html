How the model works & data sources

Each slider sets a criterion’s importance. The overall score is the weighted average of five 0–100 criterion scores; zero excludes a criterion. The default colors use the same fixed scale as you adjust preferences. Kaleidoscope adds decorative rainbow colors; scores and explanations continue to update in the details. This is an illustrative model, not a verified quality-of-life ranking or probability.

Real geography: H3 resolution-2 cells, published country boundaries, and Natural Earth city points and coastlines. City and coast scores use approximate straight-line distance from each cell’s center. City proximity does not measure transport, jobs, or services.

Illustrative layers: mild climate uses latitude alone, peaking around 32° north or south. Living costs and nature access use independent synthetic patterns to demonstrate the interface. These are not observed prices, vegetation, protected areas, or climate data.

The cells cover broad regions, averaging about 86,800 km² globally. H3 cells vary in area and the global grid includes pentagons. A country label identifies the cell’s center; a cell can cross a border or coastline. Land-center sampling omits some islands and coastal fragments. Antarctica has no suitability scores.
