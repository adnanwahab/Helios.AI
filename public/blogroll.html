            Blog votes are advisory and unverified. One blog is one voice per law, and its newest post wins. Helios reads public feeds: every 15 minutes for blogs that vote and post often, every few hours for the rest. It keeps only posts that contain a Helios line (their title, link and the line itself), and never guesses a position from anything else a post says. Lines inside quotes and code blocks are ignored. Blog votes are counted separately from the advisory votes on the law board. Free Weed for Everyone is a policy draft for adults 21 and over; nothing is provided by Helios.
