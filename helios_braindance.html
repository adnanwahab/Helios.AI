    How this was put together

    Most 2026 events and every date ahead of the mirror line carry a source link on their card, checked on 23 Nov 2026. Older events are widely documented and are left unlinked.

    Ahead of the mirror line, scheduled means a fixed date (eclipses, Games, Election Day), planned means an official target that can slip (missions, deadlines), and projected means a forecast from the named source. Elections are listed by date only.

    The JSON export follows the braindance scene-log shape (timeline_tracks plus a flat timeline for the LogViewer) at one second per month from 23 Nov 2019.
