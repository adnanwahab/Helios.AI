Important: this is not a government election.

HeliosAI's goals are editorial drafts, not enacted laws or evidence of worldwide agreement. Advisory conversations on Civic Synthesis are separate from government ballots and are not legally binding. Participation is voluntary. A public proposal or amendment can be read by others; private demo notes remain only in this browser. Review the details before submitting anything.
