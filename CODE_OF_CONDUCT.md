# Helios community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate respectfully, including people who disagree with the project's proposals. Participation and public attribution are voluntary.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and evidence rather than attacking individuals.
- Listen, ask questions, credit sources, and acknowledge mistakes and uncertainty.
- Respect personal boundaries and requests to stop contact.
- Make room for newcomers and different perspectives.
- Keep participant information private unless its publication is explicitly authorized.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, impersonation, coordinated vote manipulation, unsolicited repeated contact, and publication of private information or secrets are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For public content, use the repository or platform reporting controls as appropriate. Never post sensitive reports or identifying details in public issues. A private moderation contact and backup reviewer must be published by maintainers before public launch. For GitHub abuse, use [GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).

Maintainers should review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality as far as practicable, document decisions without exposing complainants, and provide a way to appeal. Depending on severity and platform controls, actions may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction or exclusion. Immediate safety and privacy risks may require prompt action. Reviewers must recuse themselves from conflicts of interest.

This is a community moderation policy, not a legal or official government process.

Moderation roles and independent review are described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). The reporting-contact and backup-moderator launch gate stays open until real volunteers accept the duties and a private channel is published; this policy alone does not establish that coverage.
