03 / How we work

A contribution is a conversation.

  1. 01Propose a change
  2. 02Research & build
  3. 03Simulate & visualize
  4. 04Review together
  5. 05Publish improvement
Real participants and simulated agents are separate. Simulations test assumptions; they do not measure actual public agreement. Disagreement is welcome. All submissions are voluntary, and publication is subject to review.

04 / Start in ten minutes

Make your first small change.

Our target is a first useful contribution in ten minutes, not a guaranteed review time. Credit contributors who opt in to public attribution.

Contributor guide ↗Launch roadmap ↗
  1. Pick an issue or propose a small change.
  2. Comment with what you intend to do; wait for coordination on shared files.
  3. Submit evidence, draft text, a design, or a focused pull request.
  4. Include verification and welcome review. Do not include private journals, keys or personal information.
  5. Review and revise with other contributors.
